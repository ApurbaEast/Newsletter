THE BIG STORY

02.

Bitcoin Reclaims $60,000. Inflation Keeps
Markets on Edge.

Bitcoin moved back above $60,000 on
Thursday after a volatile trading session
triggered by stronger-than-expected US
inflation data.

The October Core Personal Consumption
Expenditures (PCE) Price Index, the
Federal Reserve's preferred inflation
measure, rose 3.3% year-on-year. US
third-quarter GDP growth was also
revised upwards to 4.3%, reinforcing
expectations that interest rates could
remain elevated.

Bitcoin briefly fell towards $57,200 before
recovering above $60,000. The broader
crypto market also recorded a rebound,
although institutional fund flows continue
to indicate caution.

US spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded
approximately $4.94 billion in net
outflows between 2 and 24 November,
marking their largest monthly outflow on
record at the time of reporting.

What to watch:

Federal Reserve policy: Persistent inflation could strengthen expectations of further rate increases in December. 
Bitcoin ETF flows: Continued outflows could weigh on institutional demand. 
Market leverage: Approximately $943 million in crypto positions were liquidated over a 24-hour period, highlighting the impact of leveraged positioning. 
Bitcoin price levels: Market participants are watching the $57,000–$58,000 area as support and $61,000–$62,000 as an important recovery zone. 

The coming sessions will show whether Bitcoin's recovery can attract sustained spot demand.
